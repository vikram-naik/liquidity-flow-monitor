Indicator Reference Guide

This guide documents every marker, overlay, and data point displayed in the Divergence Engine charts. Each entry includes its definition, typical value range, and practical interpretation guidance for making informed trading decisions.

The Divergence Engine uses a multi-anchor rolling window system across four time horizons — 10, 30, 60, and 120 bars — to analyze delivery volume behaviour and its relationship with price.

📈

Price Chart Overlays

Lines and bands overlaid on the candlestick chart. These represent composite delivery-weighted price levels, acting as dynamic support/resistance zones derived from institutional participation data.

CWVAP — Composite Weighted VWAP

Overlay ▸

Definition: A delivery-weighted blend of DVWAPs (Delivery VWAPs) across all four anchor windows [10, 30, 60, 120]. Each window's DVWAP is weighted by its DVL rate and proximity to the current price, producing a single composite "fair value" line.

Formula:CWVAP = Σ(DVWAP_n × W_n) / Σ(W_n), where W_n = DVL_rate_n × (1 / (1 + |close − DVWAP_n| / ATR₂₀))
Scale:Price units (same as the stock price)

How to interpret:

Condition Signal Meaning
Price above CWVAP Bullish Price is above fair delivery value — upside momentum is being supported by institutional participation.
Price below CWVAP Bearish Price is trading below the delivery-weighted average — institutions may be holding back.
Price crossing above CWVAP Bullish Reclaim A potential inflection — delivery support is being recaptured. Watch for follow-through.
Price crossing below CWVAP Value Loss Loss of delivery support. Can trigger "losing_value" price location classification.

💡 CWVAP is the most important single line on the chart — it represents where the "smart money" consensus is.

CPOC — Composite Point of Control

Overlay ▸

Definition: The delivery-weighted average of per-window POCs. Each POC is the price level where the most delivery volume transacted within a given anchor window. Serves as an alternative "magnet" price level based on the highest delivery concentration.

Scale:Price units

How to interpret:

Condition Signal Meaning
CPOC close to CWVAP Consensus Delivery activity is concentrated around the same level — strong support/resistance zone.
CPOC diverging from CWVAP Caution The peak delivery price is shifting away from the weighted average — potential transition happening.
Price > CPOC > CWVAP Distribution risk Price location is classified as "extended_above" — a distribution zone warning.

CVAH / CVAL — Composite Value Area High / Low

Overlay ▸

Definition: The upper and lower bounds of the composite value area, displayed as thin ghost lines (dashed) on the chart. Computed as delivery-weighted extremes of per-window value-area bands. Together, CVAH and CVAL define the zone where the majority of delivery volume has transacted.

Scale:Price units

How to interpret:

Condition Signal Meaning
Price within the Value Area (CVAL–CVAH) In Value Area Price is trading within the normal delivery range — no extreme positioning.
Price above CVAH Above Value Price is extended beyond the delivery area — can signal strength or overextension.
Price below CVAL Below Value Price has fallen below the delivery zone — weakness or opportunity for accumulation.
Narrow Value Area Compression Delivery is concentrated — potential breakout setup.

DV10 / DV30 / DV60 / DV120 — Delivery VWAPs

Overlay ▸

Definition: Per-window Delivery VWAPs — the rolling volume-weighted average price over 10, 30, 60, and 120 bars respectively. Each DVWAP reveals the average cost basis of participants entering during that specific time window.

Formula:DVWAP_n = rolling_sum(TP × volume, n) / rolling_sum(volume, n)
Scale:Price units
Window Color Timeframe Signal
DV10 Light Blue Short-term (2 weeks) — fast-moving traders
DV30 Blue Medium-short (6 weeks) — swing traders
DV60 Medium Blue Medium-term (3 months) — positional players
DV120 Dark Blue Long-term (6 months) — institutional anchors

Interpretation: When DVWAPs are fanned out in ascending order (DV10 > DV30 > DV60 > DV120), it signals a healthy uptrend with each new cohort entering at higher prices. When they converge, a trend change may be developing. When they cross over, "smart money" is repositioning.

Delivery Volume — Volume Histogram

Overlay ▸

Definition: The actual shares delivered (settled) on each trading day, plotted as a histogram on the price chart. The bar colour is determined by the Money Flow Multiplier (MFM): green for positive MFM (closes in upper half of range), red for negative MFM (closes in lower half).

Color:Green = MFM ≥ 0 (bullish intraday close) · Red = MFM < 0 (bearish intraday close)

How to interpret: High delivery volume with green bars = strong institutional buying. High delivery with red bars = distribution day. Look for delivery spikes on breakout days to confirm the move has institutional support.

Chart Markers

Discrete signals plotted directly on the price candles. These appear only when specific confidence/probability thresholds are met, ensuring only actionable signals are shown.

Divergence Arrows — Bullish ↑ / Bearish ↓

Marker ▸

Definition: Arrows plotted when the Divergence Engine detects a significant mismatch between price momentum and delivery participation. The opacity of the arrow reflects the divergence probability — more opaque = higher confidence.

Trigger:Divergence probability > 50% and direction ≠ "none"
Arrow Position Signal Meaning
▲ Green Up Below bar Bullish Divergence Price is falling but delivery volume is rising — institutions may be accumulating quietly.
▼ Red Down Above bar Bearish Divergence Price is rising but delivery is declining — smart money may be distributing into strength.

Probability components:

Component Weight Details
Multi-window Confluence 30% Agreement across 10, 30, 60, 120 anchors
|MCS Composite| 30% Magnitude of money flow alignment
|PDD Weighted Avg| 20% Magnitude of Price-Delivery Divergence
(1 − CWC) 20% Inverse of Cross-Window Coherence

💡 The raw composite is passed through a sigmoid function to produce the final [0, 1] probability.

Market State Markers — 6 States

Marker ▸

Definition: Each bar is classified into one of 6 market states using weighted evidence scoring across all divergence engine metrics. A marker appears on the candlestick only when the state confidence exceeds 65%.

▲ V-Bottom Reversal

Sharp bounce supported by underlying flow. Strong early sign but needs confirmation.

▲ Value Breakout

Surging above resistance while retaining volume coherence. Significant institutional interest.

▲ Early Markup

Nascent uptrend formation indicated by short-term velocity. Early participants stepping in.

▲ Confirmed Markup

All velocity cohorts align positively with clear strength. Sustained uptrend ongoing.

Stealth Accumulation

Delivery is quietly rising while price is suppressed or stagnant. Smart money building positions.

Exhaustion Warning

Price retains highs but institutional volume dwindles. Warning of an impending top.

▼ Distribution Top

Classic top indicators where price floats but flow reveals selling. Institutions exiting.

▼ Active Distribution

Accelerating selling pressure confirmed by short-term metrics. Bearish trend initiating.

▼ Value Breakdown

Support faltering with high volume, violating value zones. Key floors breached.

▼ Confirmed Markdown

All velocity cohorts negative; full capitulation/selling. Deep persistent downtrend.

Dead Cat Bounce

Short-term price spike within broader downtrend not backed by volume. False relief rally.

Neutral / Mixed

No clear institutional direction, low alignment. Wait for setup.

Cross-Window Coherence (CWC) Panel

Measures how consistently delivery cohorts across all four anchor windows move together. High coherence confirms trends are real; low coherence signals transitions or institutional repositioning.

CWC — Cross-Window Coherence

Panel ▸

Definition: The mean of all pairwise 10-bar rolling Pearson correlations between the day-over-day changes in DVL_rate across the four anchor windows. It answers: "Are all delivery cohorts moving in the same direction?"

Range:−1.0 to +1.0 (typically oscillates between 0 and 1)
Level Signal Interpretation
> 0.70 Trend Aligned All cohorts are moving together. The current trend (up or down) is genuine and supported by delivery across all timeframes. High-confidence environment.
0.30 – 0.70 Transitioning Mixed signals between cohorts. A regime change may be developing. Proceed with caution.
< 0.30 Diverging Cohorts are moving in opposing directions — some are accumulating while others are distributing. Classic sign of smart money repositioning.
Negative Opposing Active counter-flow between cohorts — typically seen during accumulation or distribution phases where short-term and long-term participants have opposing interests.

📐 Reference lines are drawn at 0.7 (green, dashed) and 0.3 (red, dashed) on the chart.

C1030 / C3060 / C60120 — Pairwise Coherence

Panel ▸

Definition: Pairwise rolling Pearson correlations between adjacent window pairs. These decompose the aggregate CWC into its component relationships for more granular trend analysis.

Pair Color What It Compares Early Warning Use
C1030 Green 10-bar vs 30-bar cohorts Fastest signal — first to diverge when short-term sentiment shifts. If C1030 drops while C60120 stays high, it's an early warning of trend change.
C3060 Orange 30-bar vs 60-bar cohorts Mid-range confirmation. When this drops, the shift is propagating from short-term into medium-term.
C60120 Red 60-bar vs 120-bar cohorts Slowest to change — when this diverges, the long-term institutional view is shifting. Major trend changes confirmed.

Key pattern: A "cascade breakdown" (C1030 drops → C3060 drops → C60120 drops) over several weeks is a powerful regime-change signal. The reverse cascade confirms a new trend is being institutionally adopted.

Money Composite Score (MCS) Panel

Quantifies the alignment between price action and delivery participation. Answers the question: "Is the money flow supporting the price move?"

MCS Composite — Primary Money-Flow Health Score

Panel ▸

Definition: A blended correlation signal that measures how well price direction and delivery volume are aligned. Combines two Pearson correlations: typical price vs delivery (MCS) and money-flow-adjusted price vs delivery (MCS_MFM).

Formula:MCS_composite = 0.6 × MCS + 0.4 × MCS_MFM
Range:−1.0 to +1.0
Level Signal Interpretation
> +0.5 Strong Alignment Price and delivery are highly correlated — the price trend is being supported by real institutional participation.
+0.3 to +0.5 Moderate Alignment Positive relationship but not overwhelming. Look for confirming signals.
−0.3 to +0.3 Neutral No clear relationship between price and delivery. Market is noise-driven.
< −0.3 Divergent Price and delivery are moving in opposite directions — a potential divergence signal.

📐 A zero reference line is drawn on the chart.

MCS — Rolling Pearson(TP, RDV)

Panel ▸

Definition: 30-day rolling Pearson correlation between Typical Price and Relative Delivery Volume (RDV). Measures whether higher prices coincide with higher delivery or vice versa.

Range:−1.0 to +1.0

Interpretation: Positive MCS = price going up as delivery rises (genuine buying). Negative MCS = price rising while delivery falls (hollow rally) or price falling while delivery rises (accumulation). Used as 60% weight in the MCS Composite.

MFM — MCS_MFM (Rolling Pearson of MFM_TP vs RDV)

Panel ▸

Definition: 30-day rolling Pearson correlation between MFM-adjusted Typical Price and RDV. This variant incorporates intraday money flow pressure — whether the close is in the upper or lower half of the day's range.

MFM formula:MFM = ((close − true_low) − (true_high − close)) / (true_high − true_low)
Range:−1.0 to +1.0

Interpretation: Positive MFM correlation = delivery is concentrated on days where price closes strong (upper half of range). Negative MFM = delivery happens on weak closes. This adds an intraday quality dimension that raw MCS doesn't capture. Used as 40% weight in MCS Composite.

ΔM — MCS Delta (Histogram)

Panel ▸

Definition: 5-bar rate of change in MCS — the "momentum of money flow alignment." Shows whether the price-delivery relationship is improving or deteriorating.

Formula:ΔM = MCS(today) − MCS(5 days ago)
Color:Green = positive (improving) · Red = negative (deteriorating)

Interpretation: Even when MCS itself is negative, a positive (green) ΔM means it's getting less negative — things are improving. Conversely, a negative ΔM during a positive MCS warns that the alignment is weakening. ΔM often leads MCS by 3-5 bars, making it a valuable early warning signal.

Velocity Panel

Normalised rate-of-change (slope) of cumulative delivery volume for each anchor window. Velocity captures the speed of delivery accumulation — not just level but direction and strength.

V10 / V30 / V60 / V120 — Normalised DVL Velocity

Panel ▸

Definition: The rolling linear regression slope of cumulative delivery volume (DVL_n) over the last n/2 bars, normalised by the 60-bar mean of DVL_rate. This produces a directional and magnitude indicator that is comparable across stocks regardless of absolute delivery size.

Formula:V_n = slope(DVL_n, n/2) / mean(DVL_rate_n, 60)
Range:Unbounded, but typically ±2.0. Centred at 0.
Color:Green = positive · Red = negative (per bar)
Window Color Opacity Represents
V10 60% opacity Short-term delivery velocity — first to react to new buying/selling.
V30 40% opacity Medium-short — confirms whether V10 moves are sustaining.
V60 25% opacity Medium-term — reflects positional/institutional flow.
V120 15% opacity Long-term baseline — slow to change, but when it does, it's significant.

Key pattern — Velocity Gradient Vector: The arrangement of [V10, V30, V60, V120] from short to long is the "Gradient Shape" — the foundation for trend classification. See the Gradient Shapes section below.

Sidebar Annotations

The sidebar displays a concise summary of key metrics for the currently selected bar (or the latest bar). These are "at a glance" readings that synthesise the engine output.

State & Confidence — Market State Classification

Sidebar ▸

State is the predicted market state (UPTREND, DOWNTREND, ACCUMULATION, DISTRIBUTION, RECOVERY, or SIDEWAYS) — the winning label from the 6-state classifier.

Confidence is the probability assigned to the winning state (0–100%). It represents how dominant the evidence is for that state vs the others.

Confidence Interpretation
> 65% High conviction — the state marker is also displayed on the candlestick chart.
40% – 65% Moderate — the state label is suggestive but not dominant. Multiple states may be competing.
< 40% Low — ambiguous market conditions. The state label is not very reliable.

POC Spread — Distance Between CWVAP and CPOC

Sidebar ▸

Definition: The distance between CWVAP and CPOC expressed in ATR₂₀ units. Measures how far apart the "average delivery price" and the "peak delivery price" are.

Scale:ATR units (0 = perfectly aligned)
Level Signal Interpretation
< 0.5 Tight CWVAP and CPOC are close — strong consensus zone. Acts as a powerful support/resistance level.
0.5 – 1.5 Normal Moderate spread — typical market conditions.
> 1.5 Wide Delivery is dispersed — no clear institutional consensus. Often seen during transitions or choppy markets.

Divergence — Direction & Probability

Sidebar ▸

Definition: Shows the current divergence direction (bullish/bearish/none) and its probability score. This is the aggregate output of three divergence detection methods:

Detection Layer What It Compares
Price vs RDV Divergence Per-window sign mismatches between price momentum and relative delivery volume momentum.
CWVAP Divergence Price momentum outrunning or lagging the composite value line over 10 bars.
MCS Divergence MCS crossover signals when price is above/below CWVAP — detects money-flow crossovers against price positioning.
Divergence Probability Components:
Component Weight Significance
Confluence Score 30% Total windows (10, 30, 60, 120) agreeing on the divergence signal.
|MCS Composite| 30% Magnitude of Money Flow momentum — higher scores increase conviction.
|PDD Weighted Avg| 20% Intensity of delivery pressure across all timeframes.
(1 − CWC) 20% Divergence is more reliable when Trend Coherence is low/broken.

When probability > 50%, a divergence arrow is plotted on the price chart. The probability is calculated using a sigmoid function, ensuring that signals scale naturally with the underlying momentum trajectory.

Gradient Shape — Velocity Gradient Classification (9 Shapes)

Sidebar ▸

Definition: Classifies the velocity gradient vector [V10, V30, V60, V120] into one of 9 distinct pattern shapes. Each shape describes the relationship between short-term and long-term delivery momentum.

🟢 uptrend_forming

V10 > 0, V30 > 0, V60 ≤ 0, V120 ≤ 0. Short-term delivery is accelerating but longer windows haven't turned yet. Early stage of a potential new uptrend.

🟢 uptrend_mature

All velocities > 0, V10 ≥ V30 ≥ V60. Full delivery alignment across all timeframes. A strong, established uptrend with momentum cascading across all windows.

🟡 uptrend_exhausting

V10 < V30, V60 > 0, V120 > 0. Short-term velocity is fading while longer windows remain positive. The uptrend is losing its initial drive — watch for follow-through failure.

🔴 distribution

V10 < 0, V30 near 0, V60 > 0, V120 > 0. Short-term delivery is declining while long-term is still trending up. Classic distribution pattern — smart money selling into long-term strength.

🔵 accumulation

V10 > 0 and rising, V30–V120 ≤ 0. Short-term delivery is picking up against a negative longer-term backdrop. Quiet buying into weakness — early accumulation signal.

🔴 downtrend_forming

V10 < 0, V30 < 0, V60 ≥ 0. Short and medium windows declining, long-term still positive. The beginning of a potential downtrend.

🔴 downtrend_mature

All velocities < 0. Complete delivery decline across all timeframes. Established downtrend with no window showing positive velocity.

🟢 recovering

V10 > 0, V30 slope turning positive, V60 < 0. Short-term delivery is reviving against a still-negative medium-term. Earliest signal of potential trend reversal.

⚪ sideways

All velocities near zero (|v| < 0.2). No meaningful delivery direction. Range-bound — wait for a velocity breakout to signal the next direction.

Price Location — 7 Categories

Sidebar ▸

Definition: Classifies the current close price relative to the composite delivery value lines (CWVAP, CPOC, CVAH, CVAL). Used internally by the state classifier and shown in the sidebar for context.

Location Condition Interpretation
extended_above Price > CPOC > CWVAP Price has extended well above delivery consensus. Distribution risk — institutions may begin taking profits.
above_value Price > CWVAP and > CPOC Price is above both delivery lines — bullish positioning. Trend is supported.
at_value |Price − CWVAP| < 0.3 ATR Price is right at the delivery-weighted fair value. A decision point — watch for direction.
in_value_area CVAL ≤ Price ≤ CVAH Trading within the normal delivery range. No extreme positioning.
below_value Price < CWVAP and < CPOC Below delivery consensus. Weakness or potential accumulation zone.
reclaiming_value Crossed above CWVAP in last 3 bars Just reclaimed the delivery value line — a bullish inflection if sustained.
losing_value Crossed below CWVAP in last 3 bars Just lost delivery support — bearish unless quickly reclaimed.
