Conviction-Gated Marker System

The Divergence Engine uses a simple, high-precision system to identify Demand (buying pressure) and Supply (selling pressure) conditions in NSE stocks. Instead of 11 complex states, the engine applies three conviction gates to produce just two actionable markers with a confidence score.

Demand & Supply Markers

▲ Demand

Price is below the Composite VWAP (CWVAP), indicating a discount zone. All three conviction gates are satisfied, suggesting genuine institutional buying interest with sustained delivery volume.

Chart: Green arrow below the price bar.

▼ Supply

Price is above the Composite VWAP, indicating a premium zone. All three conviction gates are satisfied, suggesting institutional distribution with sustained delivery volume.

Chart: Red arrow above the price bar.

Conviction Gates

All three gates must pass simultaneously for a signal to fire:

GateConditionDefaultWhat it measures
CWC Gate CWC ≤ threshold 0.65 Cross-Window Coherence must be low enough to indicate a divergence between price and delivery patterns across timeframes.
RDV Gate RDV ≥ threshold 0.6 Relative Delivery Volume must be elevated, confirming that delivery-based participation is above average.
RDV Consistency ≥ N days in last 5 2 Number of recent days where RDV exceeded 1.0 (above-average delivery). Ensures the delivery surge is sustained, not a one-day spike.

Additionally, CWVAP Distance determines direction: negative = Demand, positive = Supply.

Conviction Score (0–100)

When a signal fires, a weighted score quantifies its strength:

ComponentWeightCalculation
CWC Score25%max(0, 1 - CWC) — lower CWC = stronger signal
RDV Score20%min(RDV / 2.0, 1.0)
RDV Consistency15%rdv_consistency / 5
CWVAP Depth15%min(|cwvap_dist| / 5.0, 1.0)
Delivery %10%min(delivery_pct / 80.0, 1.0)
PDD Score15%min(|pdd_30| / 20.0, 1.0)

Higher scores indicate stronger conviction. Scores above 50 are considered actionable for screening purposes.

Chart Overlays

CWVAP (Composite VWAP)

Multi-window volume-weighted average price. Acts as the directional anchor — price below CWVAP = discount (Demand zone), above = premium (Supply zone).

Chart Panels

Panel 1 — Price + Overlays

Candlestick chart with CWVAP overlay, VA boundaries, and delivery volume histogram.

Panel 2 — Velocity (Z-Scores)

Price Slope Z and RDV Slope Z show normalised momentum for price and delivery volume.

Panel 3 — Coherence

Raw and smoothed Cross-Window Coherence. Lower coherence combined with high RDV indicates divergence conditions favourable for signals.

Sidebar Annotations

The sidebar shows the current bar's key metrics:

  • Integrated State — Demand, Supply, or No Signal + conviction score
  • CWC — Cross-Window Coherence value
  • RDV — Relative Delivery Volume
  • RDV Consistency — Days with RDV ≥ 1.0 in last 5
  • CWVAP Dist — % distance from CWVAP
  • Delivery % — Delivery percentage
  • Coherence — Smoothed coherence value
